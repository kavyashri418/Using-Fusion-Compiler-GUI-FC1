## Layout Panning and Zooming
- Maximize the Layout View by double-clicking on the title bar of the layout tab
- Explore the zoom and pan buttons as shown below. Note that once we select zoom or pan, cursor changes to the magnifying glass or hand icon, and the tool remains in that mode
- To exit the zoom and pan mode press the [ESC] key or pick the selection tool (the arrow icon ) The cursor returns to an arrow or pointer shape
- Use hot keys: [F] or [Ctrl F] both correspond to zoom Fit All (or full view), [+] or [=] is Zoom in 2x, [-] is Zoom out 2x
- Use the mouse scroll wheel to zoom in/out (2x) around the area of the mouse's pointer
- Click and hold the middle mouse button or wheel while moving the pointer straight up or down and holding it there. The stroke menu appears near the pointer as shown below
- Release the middle button and the design should ZOOM to fit the display window (Zoom Fit All) to zoom in on an area place your cursor in the area of the interest then stroke (move the mouse with the middle button or wheel depressed) in a 45 degree direction upward (to the left or right) -the view should zoom in to a rectangular area defined by the stroke
